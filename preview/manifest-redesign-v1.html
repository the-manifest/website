Why Manifest. Why now.

The full thesis belongs in the Meet Manifest article. The website gives every visitor the essential context in under a minute—and a direct path to continue the conversation.

01 / THE MARKET

A new industrial category

Physical labour represents one of the world’s largest economic systems. Humanoids can become a new infrastructure layer for work that is difficult to staff, sustain, or scale.

02 / THE FOCUS

European defence first

Defence rewards sovereign, compliant technology and demands resilient European supply chains. It is where Manifest’s product and mission align most strongly.

03 / THE FIRST JOB

Inspection as the entry point

Inspection combines clear operational value with manageable manipulation, limited human interaction, and environments where humanoids can become productive first.
